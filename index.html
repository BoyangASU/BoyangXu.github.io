I am a 5th-year Ph.D. student in Data Science affiliated with the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence (SCAI) at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Hao Yan.

ICML 2026 · Distributional RL · Flow Matching

Path-Coupled Bellman Flows

Flow-matching critics can learn full return distributions without fixed atoms or quantiles, but forcing the Bellman equation into a flow either moves its starting point off the Gaussian prior or leaves the current and successor flows uncoupled, which makes training targets noisy. PCBF builds Bellman paths that start at the prior, end at the Bellman target, and share their noise with the successor flow, so the successor's velocity becomes a variance-reducing control variate.

The project page includes interactive demos of the path coupling and the bias–variance trade-off.

Preprint 2026 · Distributional RL · Flow Matching

Retimed Bellman Flows

Velocity bootstrapping trains a flow critic against a teacher read at the successor state, but on straight paths no same-time query can keep the Gaussian start, stay affine in the teacher and use fresh successor noise all at once: an impossible triangle. ReBF reads the teacher at a retimed flow time τ(t) < t and generates the successor from independent noise, which gives a conditionally unbiased target with no new hyperparameter and up to 7.7× lower return-law error than PCBF.

The project page includes interactive demos of the retimed query and of the full design space of clock, noise coupling and correction weight.

Retimed Bellman Flows: Escaping the Impossible Triangle of Velocity Bootstrapping
Boyang Xu, Shengzhe Chen, Hao Yan
Fifteenth International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) under review · Sep 2026
Flow critics learn return distributions by transporting Gaussian noise to Bellman endpoints via continuous velocity fields. While velocity bootstrapping stabilizes training by querying a successor teacher, existing methods face a structural dilemma: on straight paths, no residual-free same-time affine mapping can preserve Gaussian initial noise while maintaining an unbiased target. To overcome this limitation, we introduce Retimed Bellman Flows (ReBF).
